About us

Bali Are Property is an independent property agency in Bali, Indonesia, established in 2009. With extensive experience in Bali's real estate market, we specialize in property sales, land investment, land acquisition and villa opportunities, particularly across South Kuta and North Kuta.

We connect property owners with local and international buyers and investors looking for land, villas and investment opportunities in Bali.

Our market knowledge, local network and understanding of property locations help clients identify suitable properties based on their investment objectives, development plans and budget.
