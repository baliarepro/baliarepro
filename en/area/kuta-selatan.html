Land & Villas for Sale in South Kuta (Bukit), Bali

South Kuta is the Bukit peninsula at Bali's southern tip: Ungasan, Jimbaran, Pecatu–Uluwatu, Kutuh–Pandawa, Balangan and Nusa Dua. Its clifftops and beaches are home to villas, resorts and beach clubs, close to Ngurah Rai Airport via the Bali Mandara toll road and the bypass. It has been Bali Are Property's core area since 2009.
